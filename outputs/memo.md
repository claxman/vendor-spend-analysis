# Vendor spend review

**To:** the CEO and the CFO of the group
**From:** Chaitanya Laxman, VP Operations, the acquired business
**Date:** November 11, 2026
**Subject:** Vendor review: $2,229,000 a year in savings, $1,209,000 in year 1

**Bottom line.** We reviewed all 386 vendors and $7,887,000 of third-party spend for the last 12 months: the base case saves $2,229,000 a year, 28% of spend, with $1,209,000 landing in year 1. The high case is $3,097,000 if every decision below is taken and every negotiation lands, and three moves carry most of it.

**Context.** The data is the acquired business's accounts-payable total per vendor for 12 months; Salesforce alone is $3,117,000, about 40% of spend, because our product runs on its platform.

| Move | Run-rate saving | Year 1 | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exit the offices under a remote operating model, keeping one hub space | $669,000 | $335,000 | Your decision; notices to five landlords |
| Move back-office services to the group's shared functions | $653,000 | $359,000 | Your decisions; the auditor at year-end, systems after migration |
| Renegotiate the Salesforce platform agreement at change of control | $515,000 | $257,000 | The contract and usage data; no exit possible |
| Second wave: travel, software, cloud, telecom, marketing, contractors | $392,000 | $258,000 | Vendor action we take ourselves |
| Total | $2,229,000 | $1,209,000 | |

**Decisions needed**
- A remote operating model for the acquired business: worth $466,000 a year more than consolidating onto one flexible provider ourselves. Needed by December 15, 2026, so notices go out in the first quarter and year 1 holds.
- Group shared functions for audit, hiring, legal, payroll, insurance and finance systems: worth $455,000 a year more than tenders and seat reviews alone (the auditor $139,000, the talent function $133,000). Needed by January 31, 2027, ahead of the audit year-end.
- The Salesforce move needs no decision from you, only the contract and the usage data.

**Plan**
- 0 to 30 days: collect the contracts, renewal and notice dates and the Salesforce usage data; confirm the 67 payees not yet identified with Finance.
- 30 to 90 days: serve the office notices, open the Salesforce and insurance negotiations, stop the perks and card spend.
- 90 to 180 days: exit the offices, transfer hiring and legal work to the group, consolidate the software.
- 6 to 12 months: move the audit and the HR and finance systems at year-end.
- How it is run: one owner per move, a weekly tracker, and a saving counted only when the contract changes.

**Risks**
- Platform dependency: the product runs on Salesforce, so we negotiate terms and never threaten exit.
- Retention at the Zagreb engineering hub, where perks, contractors and the office all change at once.
- Notice periods and exit costs are not yet known; the office figure assumes half a year.
- Vendors that customers or regulators require stay: $171,000 of mandated fees, statutory cover and escrow.

**Not counted:** $274,000 of deal and one-off costs that fall away on their own.

**Scale.** Against group revenue the direct saving is modest; the value is a review that repeats on every acquisition, and this one found 28% of third-party spend.
