# Department taxonomy

The only departments allowed are the 12 values in the workbook's `Config` sheet, spelled exactly as there and listed here in the same order. The workbook's Department column (B2:B387) is a dropdown validated against `Config!$A$2:$A814` with the error alert on, so any other spelling would be rejected in the workbook. `scripts/02_clean.py` checks that the table below and `Config` agree, character for character.

A department answers one question: whose budget is this? The cost cluster (`docs/clusters.md`) answers another: which lever captures the saving? The two can differ (decision of 2026-11-10, `docs/decisions.md`). SaaS is read as one of the functions of a software company, the one that runs the hosted product, not as a label for any software subscription (decision of 2026-11-11, step 3c). Slack is G&A by department, as corporate IT (step 3c), and Salesforce platform by cluster, because it is Salesforce-owned and goes into the same renewal. Cloudcrossing's PDF Butler is SaaS by department, as a component delivered with the hosted product (step 3c), and Salesforce platform by cluster. Mason Frank is G&A by department, as a recruiting agency, and from step 3b Recruiting and HR outsourcing by cluster, because its lever is ending the agency, not the Salesforce contract.

Recommendations follow `docs/recommendation_rules.md`; analyst knowledge about specific vendors is in `docs/vendor_hints.md`.

## Assignment rules

These are the rules as given (prompt 6 in `prompts/log.md`), with rule 1 amended in steps 3b and 3c (prompts 16 and 17), rule 2 rewritten in step 3c, and rules 7, 9 and 10 following them.

1. Function-specific software goes to the function that uses it: HubSpot, Uberflip, Semrush to Marketing; Aha!, Figma to Product; Sage, Planful to Finance; Peakon, Performancepro to G&A as HR software; Kimble to Professional Services. Salesforce (amended in steps 3b and 3c): the Salesforce platform licences and the components embedded in or delivered with the product go to SaaS (rule 2); Salesforce-ecosystem vendors that supply product engineering go to Engineering; only internal-CRM tooling goes to Sales.
2. SaaS is the function that runs the hosted product (rewritten in step 3c; the prompt 6 wording is in `prompts/log.md`). It holds the platform and hosting the product runs on and the tools that keep it running: platform and ISV licences, cloud hosting, monitoring and logging, DNS and domains, and third-party components delivered with the product. Company-wide software with no single owning function (productivity suites, messaging, meetings, e-signature, password management, automation and work-management tools) goes to G&A, with a sub_function that starts "Corporate IT". Software used by one function stays with that function, and developer tools stay in Engineering.
3. Professional Services means the company's own services-delivery department, not external advisers: PSA software, customer training and certification delivery, subcontractors on customer projects. External advisers go to Finance, Legal, M&A or G&A by what they do.
4. Finance: audit, tax, accounting, R&D tax credit advisers, finance systems, payment processing, bank fees, share registry and depository services.
5. M&A: deal advisers, data rooms, sponsor and transaction fees.
6. Legal: law firms, notaries, IP, immigration services, data-protection registration, corporate secretarial and registered-agent services.
7. G&A: HR (recruiting agencies, LinkedIn, job boards, payroll and employer-of-record providers, HR software), insurance and benefits (insurers, brokers, meal cards, gym, cycle-to-work, novated leasing, wellness, occupational health), travel and entertainment (Navan, airlines, hotels, restaurants, team events, gifts), training, employee IT equipment, couriers and postage, government and statutory payments, individual and unidentified payees, consumer retail purchases; from step 3c also corporate IT, the company-wide software of rule 2, with a sub_function that starts "Corporate IT".
8. Facilities: leases and coworking, property services, utilities, telecom and internet lines, parking, security, cleaning, waste, storage, movers, office supplies, furniture, office groceries and catering.
9. Engineering: developer tools, outsourced development and IT services, software escrow, Croatian sole-trader or d.o.o. contractors whose names suggest technical work, student labour contracts, and the Salesforce-ecosystem vendors that supply product engineering (rule 1). Cloud hosting, domains, monitoring and the Salesforce platform licences were here until step 3c and are SaaS from then (rule 2).
10. Sales also holds customer-mandated procurement platform fees (Telefónica Global Services, Telefónica Compras Electrónicas, SAP Ariba). Of the Salesforce ecosystem it holds only internal-CRM tooling (rule 1, step 3b).
11. Marketing holds agencies, media, PR, events, sponsorships, industry memberships (TM Forum), swag and print, stock assets.
12. Support holds support tooling and telephony for support lines.
